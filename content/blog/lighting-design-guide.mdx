Lighting is one of the fastest ways to improve a room. It changes color, mood, scale, and comfort before a single piece of furniture is replaced.

## Quick Answer

Every room needs three layers: ambient light for general brightness, task light for specific activities, and accent light for mood or architectural detail.

## Ambient Light

This is the room's overall illumination. It may come from ceiling fixtures, recessed lights, or indirect light. Keep it soft enough that the room does not feel flat.

## Task Light

Task lighting supports reading, cooking, grooming, or working. Think table lamps, sconces, under-cabinet lights, and desk lamps.

## Accent Light

Accent lighting adds depth. Use it to highlight artwork, shelves, textured walls, or a beautiful plant.

<Callout>
If a room feels expensive at night, the lighting is usually doing the work.
