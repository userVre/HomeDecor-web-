Japandi design blends Japanese restraint with Scandinavian warmth. It is minimal, but not cold. The best Japandi rooms feel quiet, natural, and deeply comfortable.

## Quick Answer

Use low furniture, warm wood, soft neutrals, natural fibers, simple ceramics, and plenty of empty space. Avoid clutter, glossy finishes, and overly decorative pieces.

## Keep the Palette Soft

Japandi rooms often use warm white, oatmeal, sand, charcoal, and natural wood. The palette should feel grounded rather than bright.

## Choose Low, Simple Shapes

Low sofas, simple tables, and rounded forms keep the room calm. Avoid furniture with too many visual details.

## Add Texture Instead of Pattern

Linen, wool, paper, wood grain, clay, and stone create interest without noise.
