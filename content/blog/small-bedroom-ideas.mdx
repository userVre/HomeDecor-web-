A small bedroom does not need to feel compromised. It needs fewer decisions, better proportions, and storage that does not dominate the room.

## Quick Answer

Choose a bed with a simple frame, use wall-mounted or narrow bedside tables, keep the palette quiet, and add vertical storage wherever possible. Let lighting and textiles create the luxury.

## Keep the Bed Visually Light

Heavy bed frames can make a small room feel smaller. A simple upholstered headboard, slim legs, or a low platform bed keeps the room open.

If the bed must include storage, choose drawers that are easy to access and avoid anything that blocks the walking path.

## Make the Walls Work

Small bedrooms benefit from vertical thinking. Wall sconces free the bedside table, tall curtains stretch the room, and high shelves keep useful items off the floor.

## Edit the Palette

Use one calm base color, one natural texture, and one accent. Too many contrasts can make the room feel busy.

<Callout>
The goal is not to make the bedroom look empty. It is to make every object earn its place.
