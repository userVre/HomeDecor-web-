A good home office needs more than a desk. It needs focus, storage, light, and a background that looks calm on video calls.

## Quick Answer

Place the desk near natural light, add a proper task lamp, reduce visible clutter, use closed storage, and choose a backdrop that feels intentional but not distracting.

## Desk Placement

Avoid facing a blank wall if it makes the room feel tight. If possible, place the desk so you can see the door or a window without glare on the screen.

## Storage Is the Secret

Open shelves can look good, but closed storage keeps the room calm. Use boxes, cabinets, or drawers for papers and tech accessories.

## Build a Better Background

A framed artwork, bookcase, textured wall, or plant can make the room look considered during video calls.

<Callout>
