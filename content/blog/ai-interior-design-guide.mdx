AI interior design works best when it has a clear job. If the prompt is vague, the result usually looks polished but generic. If the goal is specific, the result becomes much more useful.

## Quick Answer

Start with a real room photo, name the room function, choose one style direction, describe what must stay, and ask for realistic materials, lighting, and furniture scale.

## Give the Room Context

Tell the tool how the room is used. A family living room, rental bedroom, home office, and guest room all need different decisions.

## Avoid Style Overload

Do not ask for "modern luxury Japandi Parisian cozy minimal" in one prompt. Pick one primary direction and one supporting mood.

## Ask for Practical Constraints

Useful prompts mention budget, existing floor color, window placement, storage needs, pets, children, or rental restrictions.
