Open-plan rooms can feel generous, but they can also feel vague. The goal is to create zones without closing the room down.

## Quick Answer

Use rugs, lighting, furniture backs, dining placement, and repeated materials to define each area. Every zone should have a purpose and a visual anchor.

## Let Furniture Create Boundaries

A sofa can act like a soft wall between the living area and dining area. A console behind the sofa can make that boundary feel more intentional.

## Use Rugs With Confidence

Rugs are one of the easiest ways to create zones. Use a living room rug under the seating group and let the dining area stand on its own or use a different texture.

## Repeat Materials

Open-plan rooms need connection. Repeat wood tones, black accents, or fabric colors so each zone feels related.

<Callout>
An open-plan space should feel connected, not like one large room with furniture floating in it.
</Callout>
