An expensive-looking room is not always an expensive room. Often, it is a room with better lighting, cleaner proportions, and fewer mismatched pieces.

## Quick Answer

Rearrange the layout, upgrade lighting, add larger curtains, use one oversized artwork, simplify the color palette, and replace small clutter with fewer stronger objects.

## Start With What You Own

Before buying anything, move the furniture. Pull seating off the wall, center the rug, and create a clear focal point. A better layout can make old furniture feel new.

## Upgrade the Light

Harsh overhead lighting makes almost every room feel cheaper. Add a floor lamp, table lamp, or warm bulb to soften the mood.

## Use Bigger Textiles

Curtains hung higher, a rug that fits the seating area, and fuller bedding can change the scale of a room immediately.

<Callout>
Budget design works best when you spend on scale, not quantity.
