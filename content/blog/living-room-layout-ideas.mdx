The best living room layout starts with the way people move through the room. Before choosing furniture, mark the walking paths, the main view, and the place where conversation naturally happens.

## Quick Answer

Place the largest sofa first, anchor it with a rug, then add chairs so every seat has a clear relationship to the focal point. Keep walkways open and avoid pushing every piece against the walls unless the room is very narrow.

## Start With the Anchor

Most living rooms need one main anchor: a sofa, sectional, fireplace, media wall, or large window. Once the anchor is clear, the rest of the furniture can respond to it.

For a TV room, keep the sofa directly facing the screen. For a conversation room, pull chairs inward and let the coffee table become the center.

## Use the Rug to Define the Zone

A rug should be large enough to touch the front legs of the main seating pieces. This makes the room feel intentional instead of scattered.

<Callout>
If a living room feels unfinished, the problem is often scale, not style.
</Callout>

Use HomeDecor AI to preview several layouts before lifting furniture. Try one formal arrangement, one relaxed arrangement, and one open layout. The strongest option is usually the one that makes movement feel effortless.
