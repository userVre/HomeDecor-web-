When a room feels wrong, the problem is often not the style. It is usually scale, lighting, layout, or too many unrelated choices competing for attention.

## Quick Answer

The most common mistakes are buying rugs that are too small, hanging curtains too low, relying only on overhead light, using furniture that is the wrong scale, and adding decor without a clear palette.

## Small Rugs

A rug should connect the furniture. If it floats alone in the middle of the room, the seating group feels disconnected.

## Poor Lighting

One ceiling light cannot create atmosphere. Add lamps at different heights and use warm bulbs where possible.

## Too Many Accents

Accent colors work best when repeated with intention. If every object is a different color, the room feels unsettled.

## Wrong Scale

Tiny furniture can make a room feel temporary. Oversized pieces can block movement. Measure first, then buy.

<Callout>
A room becomes elegant when the big decisions are right and the small decisions are edited.
</Callout>
